# Privacy notice

> **Draft for review, not yet in force.** Text in square brackets is for the owner to fill in before the first bookkeeper uses Kompleto. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Last updated: [date it takes effect]

Kompleto helps bookkeepers in the Philippines collect documents from their clients. This notice explains what personal data Kompleto handles, why, where it goes and how long it stays, as required by the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

## 1. Who we are

Kompleto is run by [owner's full name], a sole proprietor, at [business address] ("we"). Our data protection officer is [owner's full name], reachable at [help@getkompleto.com](mailto:help@getkompleto.com).

## 2. Our two roles

- **For bookkeepers who use Kompleto** (each account is a "Firm"), we decide how the Firm's account data is used, so we are its **personal information controller**.
- **For the Firm's clients and their contact people,** the Firm decides whose data goes into Kompleto and why. The Firm is the **controller**, and we are its **personal information processor**: we handle that data only to do what the Firm asks, under our [data processing agreement](/data-processing).

If you are a bookkeeper's client and have a question about your data, ask your bookkeeper first. We will help them answer you.

## 3. What we handle

**About someone requesting an account:** the firm name and email address typed on the sign-up page, and when they accepted the terms. The request is deleted once it is approved or declined.

**About the Firm:**

- the Firm's name, and the email addresses of its owner and staff, who log in;
- when someone logged in, and a record of what was done in the account (the activity log);
- settings such as reminder days and cut-offs.

**About the Firm's clients, entered or sent by the Firm or the client:**

- business names, contact names and email addresses;
- which BIR forms the client files and which documents it still owes;
- names of the businesses that issue the client BIR Form 2307 ("Payors");
- reminder emails the Firm approved, and whether they were delivered or bounced;
- **files the client uploads**, such as receipts, invoices, bank and e-wallet statements and Form 2307s. These can contain sensitive personal information, such as tax identification numbers;
- replies the client sends to a reminder, and their photo and PDF attachments.

We don't ask for or keep government ID numbers, passwords or payment card details. We don't sell personal data, show advertising or use anyone's data to train AI.

## 4. Why we use it

- To send the reminders the Firm approved, each with the client's private upload link. **Nothing is sent unless someone at the Firm approves it.**
- To show the Firm what each client still owes, what came in, and who replied.
- To email the Firm its login links and a notice when a client replies.
- To keep Kompleto secure and working: scanning every uploaded file for viruses, limiting repeated login attempts, and keeping a record of actions.

For the Firm's own data, our basis is our contract with the Firm. For its clients' data, the Firm's basis is its own engagement with each client, and we process on its instructions.

## 5. AI

If AI wording is switched on, Kompleto asks an AI model to word reminders in Taglish or English. The model is sent only the client's business name, the contact's first name, the list of documents owed, the dates, and the language and tone. **It never receives uploaded files, their contents, tax identification numbers or amounts.** The model's provider does not train on what we send it. Without AI wording, reminders use fixed templates.

## 6. Who helps us (sub-processors)

- **[Oracle Cloud (Singapore region), or the owner's computer in the Philippines]** runs Kompleto and its database and stores the encrypted files.
- **Resend** (United States) sends reminder and login emails.
- **Cloudflare** (global network) receives client replies and connects visitors to Kompleto securely.
- **Google Drive** keeps encrypted nightly backups; Google can't read them.
- **Anthropic** (United States) words reminders, only if AI wording is on (section 5).

We tell Firms before adding or changing a sub-processor. Wherever they are, we stay responsible for how they handle the data.

## 7. How we protect it

- Each uploaded file is encrypted with its own key, and files are scanned for viruses before they are kept.
- Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Logins use one-time email links instead of passwords.
- Each Firm sees only its own data. Clients can upload files but can't see, download or delete them.
- Backups are encrypted before they leave the server.
- Every action in a Firm's account is recorded in its activity log.

## 8. How long we keep it

- **Uploaded files:** deleted 30 days after the Firm marks them received, and never kept longer than 90 days.
- **Reply text:** deleted after 90 days; only the date of the reply stays.
- **A closed account:** everything is deleted 7 days after the Firm closes it.
- **Backups:** each is deleted after 30 days, so deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days too.

The Firm can download all its data at any time from Settings.

## 9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to object, to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it erased or blocked, to receive a copy in a common format (data portability), and to claim damages.

- **Bookkeepers:** write to [help@getkompleto.com](mailto:help@getkompleto.com), or use Settings to download your data or close your account.
- **A bookkeeper's clients:** ask your bookkeeper, who controls your data; you can also write to us and we will pass your request on and help.

We answer within 15 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the National Privacy Commission at [privacy.gov.ph](https://privacy.gov.ph).

## 10. If something goes wrong

If personal data is exposed, we tell the affected Firms within 24 hours of finding out, so they can notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within the 72 hours the law allows.

## 11. Changes

We'll email Firms at least 15 days before a change to this notice takes effect, and show the date of the latest change at the top.
