# Data processing agreement

> **Draft for review, not yet in force.** Text in square brackets is for the owner to fill in. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Last updated: [date it takes effect]

This agreement is between the bookkeeping firm or bookkeeper using Kompleto (the "Firm") and [owner's full name], a sole proprietor at [business address], who runs Kompleto ("we"). It forms part of the [terms of service](/terms) and covers the personal data the Firm puts into Kompleto, or has its clients send to it. It follows section 44 of the Implementing Rules and Regulations of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

For that data, the Firm is the **personal information controller** and we are its **personal information processor**.

## 1. What we process

- **Purpose:** helping the Firm collect documents from its clients: keeping client contact details and checklists, sending reminders the Firm approves, receiving uploaded files and replies, and showing the Firm what is still missing.
- **Whose data:** the contact people at the Firm's clients; people named in the documents those clients upload, including the clients' employees in payroll documents; and the Firm's own staff who log in.
- **What data:** names, email addresses and business names; which BIR forms each client files; reminders and replies; and uploaded files such as receipts, invoices, bank and e-wallet statements, Form 2307s, timesheets and other payroll documents, and contribution payment receipts, which can contain sensitive personal information such as tax identification numbers and government ID numbers.
- **How long:** while the Firm uses Kompleto, and until its data is deleted as section 7 describes.

## 2. Only on the Firm's instructions

We process the data only on the Firm's documented instructions: this agreement, the terms of service, and what the Firm does in Kompleto, such as adding a client or approving a reminder. That includes sending data abroad, which happens only through the sub-processors in section 5. **If we think an instruction breaks the Data Privacy Act or other law, we tell the Firm at once** and don't follow it until it's resolved.

## 3. Confidentiality

Only [owner's full name] has access to the systems that hold the data. Anyone else given access in future will first be bound in writing to keep it confidential.

## 4. Security

We keep these measures in place, and will only replace one with something at least as strong:

- each uploaded file is encrypted with its own key, bound to the file and the Firm;
- every uploaded file is scanned for viruses before it is kept, and refused if it can't be scanned;
- each Firm can reach only its own data, enforced in the application's code and tested;
- **no message is sent without the Firm's approval**, and each approved message is sent at most once;
- connections use HTTPS; logins use one-time email links that expire after 15 minutes;
- the database accepts connections only from the server itself;
- an activity log records what is done in each Firm's account;
- backups are encrypted before they leave the server, and kept 30 days;
- an AI model, if used, never receives uploaded files, their contents, tax identification numbers or amounts.

## 5. Sub-processors

By accepting this agreement, the Firm authorises us to use these sub-processors:

- **[Oracle Cloud (Singapore region), or the owner's computer in the Philippines]:** runs Kompleto and stores its data.
- **Resend** (United States): sends reminder and login emails.
- **Cloudflare** (global network): receives client replies and connects visitors securely.
- **Google Drive:** keeps encrypted backups that Google can't read.
- **Anthropic** (United States): words reminders, only if AI wording is switched on, and only with the data allowed in section 4.

We'll email the Firm at least 15 days before adding or replacing one. If the Firm objects, it can close its account before the change. We stay responsible to the Firm for how each sub-processor handles the data.

## 6. Helping the Firm

- **Requests from the people concerned:** if someone asks us directly to see, correct or delete their data, we pass the request to the Firm within 3 working days and help it answer. The Firm can export everything, correct client details, and remove clients itself.
- **Breaches:** if personal data is exposed, **we tell the Firm within 24 hours** of finding out, with what we know, and keep it updated. That leaves the Firm time to notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within the 72 hours the law allows.
- **Compliance:** we give the Firm the information it needs for its own privacy impact assessments and for questions from the National Privacy Commission.

## 7. When the Firm leaves

The Firm can download all its data from Settings at any time. When it closes its account, sending stops at once and **all its data is deleted 7 days later**; copies in backups are gone within 30 days. We confirm the deletion in writing on request. Even before then, uploaded files are deleted 30 days after the Firm marks them received, and after 90 days at most.

## 8. Checking that we comply

We answer the Firm's written questions about how we handle its data, and allow the Firm, or an auditor it appoints, or the National Privacy Commission, to check our compliance with reasonable notice.

## 9. What the Firm does

The Firm puts into Kompleto only data it is allowed to process, tells its clients that it uses Kompleto (it may link to our [privacy notice](/privacy)), and gets their agreement to it, for example with a clause like this in its engagement letter:

> "We use Kompleto, a document-collection service, to send you reminders and receive your documents. Your files are encrypted, scanned for viruses and deleted within 90 days. Kompleto processes your data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement that follows the Data Privacy Act."
