# Help

Kompleto chases your clients for their documents, so you don't have to. This page explains how it works, step by step. Questions not answered here: write to [help@getkompleto.com](mailto:help@getkompleto.com).

## Getting started

1. **Add your clients.** Go to **Clients → Add client**, or **Import** a spreadsheet of all of them at once.
2. **Check each client's documents.** Open a client to see the documents Kompleto will ask for each month or quarter, and change the list if you need to.
3. **Approve the drafts.** Each morning at 7:00, Kompleto prepares the reminder emails due that day. Read them on the **Drafts** page and approve them. Nothing is sent until you do.
4. **Your clients upload.** Each email has a private link where the client uploads photos or PDFs from their phone, with no app and no login.
5. **Download and mark received.** Files appear on the client's page. Download them, then click **Mark received** on each.

Reminders stop once a client has sent everything, or replies to a reminder.

## Adding clients

For each client you give the business name, the contact's first name, their email, the reminder language (Taglish or English) and the BIR forms they file. Kompleto works out the deadlines and the documents to ask for from the forms.

**Import** takes a `.csv` or `.xlsx` file with the columns Business name, Contact first name, Email, Language and Forms, up to 500 rows. Download the template on the Import page to start. You'll see which rows are fine and which have problems before anything is added.

## How reminders are scheduled

Each client gets up to four emails for each month or quarter:

- a **first request** the day after the period ends;
- **reminders** 7, 3 and 1 day before your **cut-off**.

The cut-off is the date you want everything by. It is 5 days before the earliest BIR deadline for that period, unless you change it. On a client's **Schedule** tab you can move their cut-off, choose different reminder days, or pause their reminders. Changes affect only drafts not yet made.

## Approving drafts

The **Drafts** page lists every email waiting for you, nearest cut-off first.

- **Approve** sends one. To approve several, tick them and choose **Review selected**: you see them all once more before they go.
- **Edit** changes the subject or the text. The client's upload link must stay in it, and it can't gain other links.
- **Discard** throws a draft away.
- **Copy for Viber** copies the message, link included, to paste into your own Viber chat with the client. Copying doesn't approve or send anything.

A draft not approved by the end of its cut-off day expires, since it would arrive too late to help.

Kompleto sends at most 90 emails a day. If more are approved, those with the nearest cut-off go first and the rest go the next morning.

## What your client sees

The link in each email opens a page with your firm's name and the documents still owed. The client picks what each file is and uploads it; **2307s** also ask which business issued them. They can upload photos or PDFs of up to 10 MB each. Every file is checked for viruses first.

The client can't see, download or delete what they uploaded, and the link works only for that client and that period. It stops working 30 days after the cut-off.

## Uploads

Uploaded files are listed on the client's page under **Uploads**. **Download** saves a file with a clear name; **Mark received** records that you have it. Once the client has uploaded every document for a period, that period's remaining reminders are cancelled.

Kompleto is not an archive: **files are deleted 30 days after you mark them received, and after 90 days in any case.** Save what you need to keep in your own records.

## Replies

When a client replies to a reminder, you get an email saying so, and the reply appears on the client's page under **Replies**. A reply stops that period's remaining reminders, so you can follow up yourself. Photos and PDFs attached to a reply are kept as uploads.

Automatic replies, such as "out of office", are shown but don't stop reminders.

## Bounced emails

If a client's email address doesn't work, the dashboard shows **Email bounced** for that client. Correct the address on the client's page; new reminders go to the new address.

## The 2307 tracker

List the businesses that give a client Form 2307 (its **Payors**) on the client's **Documents** tab. The **2307 tracker** then shows, for every client, which Payors' 2307s have arrived and which are still missing.

## BIR calendar

The **BIR calendar** shows the deadlines Kompleto uses. It moves deadlines that fall on weekends and holidays, but the BIR sometimes extends them. You can enter an extended date for a client on its **Deadlines** tab. Always check against the BIR's own announcements.

## Activity

The **Activity** page lists everything done in your account over the last 90 days: approvals, emails sent, uploads, replies, logins and changes to clients.

## Staff

The person who signed up is the firm's **owner**. In **Settings → Staff**, the owner invites up to 5 staff by email. Each gets their own login link and works on the same clients and drafts. The activity log shows who did what.

Staff do the daily work: clients, drafts, approving, uploads and replies. Only the owner can invite or remove staff, download all data or close the account. Removing someone logs them out at once.

## Your data and your account

- **Download all data** in **Settings** (owner only) gives you a zip file with your clients, messages, uploads, replies and activity.
- **Close your account** in Settings stops all sending at once and deletes everything 7 days later. Write to us within those 7 days if you closed it by mistake.

See the [privacy notice](/privacy) for how your clients' data is protected.

## Questions

**Does Kompleto file returns or compute tax?** No. It only collects documents. Your books and filings stay with you.

**What do "Template" and "AI" on a draft mean?** "Template" drafts use fixed wording; "AI" drafts were worded by an AI model, which never sees your clients' files or tax data. Either way, you approve the exact text.

**Can my clients see each other's files?** No. Each link works for one client and one period, and only you can download files.

**Why didn't a reminder go out?** It may still be waiting for approval on the Drafts page, or it expired at its cut-off, or the client's reminders are paused, or they already replied or sent everything.
