# Stay outside BIR's computerized-accounting rules

Kompleto never:
- keeps books;
- computes tax;
- files returns;
- produces SAWT or other BIR-format files;
- records or issues transactions or 2307s;
- keeps documents longer than 90 days.

Under RMO 9-2021 and RMC 64-2021, software that generates books, accounting records or BIR-required reports, or that preserves books, needs CAS/CBA registration by each taxpayer who uses it. Filing and payment services need eTSP accreditation (`docs/research/p1-kill-checks.md` §2). Staying a document chaser keeps Kompleto outside both. Features that look like natural additions, such as "export to SAWT", "generate the 2307" or "keep the archive", cross that line and need a new decision with a fresh regulatory check.
