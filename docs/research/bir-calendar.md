## Weekends, holidays and extensions

- **Roll-forward.** An act due on a holiday "may be done on the next succeeding business day": Administrative Code (EO 292) Book I s.28, https://www.officialgazette.gov.ph/1987/07/25/executive-order-no-292-book-ichapter-7-regular-holidays-and-nationwide-special-days/ (OL / H). RMC 65-2016 applies this to BIR returns falling on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday (copy: https://www.grantthornton.com.ph/globalassets/1.-member-firms/philippines/tax-alerts/2016/06.27.2016/rmc-no-65-2016.pdf, OB via SA / M). PwC's 2026 calendar moves Q3 1601-EQ from 31 Oct to 3 Nov 2026, https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/client-accounting-services/2026-tax-calendar/2026-november-tax-calendar.html (SA / H).
- **Holidays.** 2026: Proclamation No. 1006 (3 Sep 2025), https://lawphil.net/executive/proc/proc2025/proc_1006_2025.html (OL / H). 2027: Proclamation No. 1427 (8 Sep 2026), news only, e.g. GMA, https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/1001999/malaca-ang-bares-holidays-for-2027/story (N / M); Official Gazette text not found. Eid'l Fitr and Eid'l Adha 2027 not yet proclaimed.
- **BIR's own calendar.** RMC 110-2025 launched an interactive 2026 tax calendar (Global Law Experts, https://globallawexperts.com/bir-launches-first-ever-interactive-digital-tax-calendar-for-2026/, SA / M). A 2027 BIR calendar: not found as of 2026-10-21.
- **Extensions** come by RMC, often, and are mostly regional: e.g. RMC 101-2025 (Typhoon Uwan, NCR and several regions), RMC 62-2026 (RDOs 110–111). Nationwide example: RMC 30-2026 moved the annual ITR from 15 Apr to 15 May 2026. Sources: PwC tax alerts, https://www.pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/tax-alerts/2026/tax-alert-12.html (SA / H).

## Owed documents

No official BIR list of documents a bookkeeper collects from a client was found. The default checklist in `docs/product/p1-spec.md` is a synthesis (recommendation, not a sourced standard) from:

- Invoices replace official receipts from 2024: RR 7-2024 under the EOPT Act; PwC, https://pwc.com/ph/en/tax/tax-publications/taxwise-or-otherwise/2024/compliance-change-on-invoicing-under-eopt.html; KPMG, https://kpmg.com/ph/en/home/insights/2024/07/compliance-an-update-to-registration-procedures-and-invoicing-requirements.html (SA / H).
- Monthly intake of sales and purchase invoices, receipts and bank statements: Triple i Consulting, https://www.tripleiconsulting.com/how-bookkeeping-services-ensure-tax-compliance-philippines/ (SB / M).
- 2307s received support 1701Q/1702Q and VAT/percentage-tax credits: Taxumo (V / M); JuanTax, https://juan.tax/form-2307 (V / H); KPMG InTax Mar 2025 (SA / H).
- 1601-C inputs are the payroll register and SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG reports: PayrollHero (V / M).
- 1601-EQ's alphalist of payees is built from the 2307s the client issued: JuanTax, https://juan.tax/qap/ (V / M).
